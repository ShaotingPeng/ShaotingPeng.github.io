Proposed Self-Labeled Preference Transfer (SPT), which jointly trains a VLA to follow and recognize execution preferences on labeled source tasks, then freezes the recognizer to self-label demonstrations of new tasks, raising preference following from 59.4% to 83.8% in simulation and from 52.5% to 92.5% on a real robot without extra annotation.
