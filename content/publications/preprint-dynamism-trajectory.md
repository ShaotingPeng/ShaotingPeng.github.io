Proposed a coarse-to-fine factorization for flow-matching VLA policies that encodes coarse motion intent into the source noise of the action expert, so a learned head, a human operator, or an agentic planner can steer drone trajectories beyond the demonstrated routes, raising simulated gate-navigation success from 45% to 73%.
